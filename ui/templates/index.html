How to use our humanizer

Four steps from a rough AI draft to something that reads like you wrote it.

1

Paste or upload your draft

Drop AI-generated text into the box above, or use "Upload file" / "Sample text" to try it instantly. Inputs are capped at {{ word_cap }} words per run.

2

Click Humanize

We run your text through several rewriting passes and keep the version that reads most naturally, benchmarked against our detector.

3

Check the score and similarity

Review the AI-likeness gauge, similarity-to-original percentage, and readability rating before you decide what to keep.

4

Copy and edit as needed

Use "Copy" to grab the rewritten text, then give it a final read-through — it's a starting point, not a final draft.
